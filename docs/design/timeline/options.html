Three ways to put your career and projects on one timeline

Each one would replace the "Things I've made" row on the homepage and sits on the same green. They are live, so hover, tap and swipe. All three use the dates from your About page and projects list.

A · The chart my pick

Jobs, projects and writing as three layers on one axis from 2007 to now. Tap any bar to read about it.

Career and projects

Best for
Seeing the whole shape at once: which projects overlapped which jobs, and that the writing peaked during the consulting years. About half the height of the current projects row.
Costs
On a phone the chart scrolls sideways. Screenshots only appear one at a time, so projects get less visual weight than they have now.

B · The spine

A vertical line, newest first. Jobs sit on the left in big type; the things you made hang off the right like the margin notes on your essays.

Career and projects

    Best for
    Reading as a story, and tying the homepage to the essay page's spine and margin notes. Works well on a phone.
    Costs
    It is tall: roughly two laptop screens, on a homepage that is already long. Durations and overlaps are not visible, only start dates.

    C · The reel

    The row you have now, put in date order with jobs added as solid cards between the project screenshots. Swipe back through time.

    Career and projects

    Best for
    Keeping the screenshots prominent and the swipe feel you liked. The smallest change from the live homepage.
    Costs
    Only three or four cards are visible at a time, so 2007 to 2014 stays hidden until someone swipes. It reads as a carousel more than a timeline.

    Two things to check. The Strategic Independent is dated 2018 because that is when its page was added to the site; correct me if the book has a better date. Quotebacks, The NEW MBA and the book are shown as single years because the projects list gives no end date.
