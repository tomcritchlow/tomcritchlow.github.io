Where full-width swiping could go

Six places on the site where a phone visitor could swipe through full-screen cards. Each frame is live: swipe it on a phone, or drag it or use the arrows on a computer. Everything in them is your real content.

What I would build: the first three. Top posts and projects on the homepage, and keep reading at the end of a post. They are the same card component in three places, and each sits where a phone reader is deciding what to look at next. The blogchain deck is a good fourth once blogchains get their own pages. I would not do the writing index.
