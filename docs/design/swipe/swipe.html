Writing index: by year

The writing index as one screen per year: swipe sideways to move through time, scroll down within a year.

Why here
Makes the shape of 14 years of writing something you can feel.
Cost
The weakest fit. A long list is quicker on a phone, and sideways-plus-downward scrolling is easy to get lost in.

On the real site these would only apply at phone width; wider screens keep the rows and lists they have now. Project screenshots are of the live sites. Library cards have no covers because most saved items do not include one.
