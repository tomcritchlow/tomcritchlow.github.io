KField & Margin

Retina Bayer · Sans + Mono

The sharpest evolution of Green Stacks. A sub-pixel Canvas glyph-matrix headline replaces raw # blocks, a live Bayer / Contour / Strata switcher sits right on the shoreline, and essays get a 6px continuous green architectural spine.

What wins
Closest to Tom's Green Stacks instincts while dramatically sharper visually; the interactive Bayer/Contour/Strata switcher lets you feel three pixel fades live; dark mode pops.
Tradeoff
Keeps sans-serif body text (Hanken Grotesk) throughout, so it leans slightly more "crisp technical blog" than "literary paperback".

Risograph · Koi Pond · Serif

Pushes the "literary, quirky" pole. Bold grotesque + italic Literata cover with an interactive ASCII Koi Pond, a sculptural variable-dot Risograph halftone fade, and a full green chapter frontispiece on every essay.

MLiving Garden

2012–2026 Skyline · Swiss Tech

Makes the pixelated fade meaningful: every ridge from 2012 to 2026 reflects your actual post count per year (hover to inspect, click to search that year). Essays get a live pixel-dither scroll seam and a quiet left TOC rail.
